# Residential Lease Agreement (Sample)

This is a fictional sample document included with DocMind for demonstration. All names, addresses and amounts are invented.

## 1. Parties

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made on March 1, 2026 between Harborview Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord"), with its office at 410 Canal Street, Suite 200, Riverton, and Maya Okafor and Daniel Reyes (together, the "Tenant").

The Landlord's property manager is Priya Natarajan, who can be reached at the office address above during business hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

## 2. Premises

The Landlord leases to the Tenant the apartment located at 88 Willow Lane, Unit 4B, Riverton (the "Premises"), including one assigned parking space (space number 17) and one storage locker in the basement.

The Premises are leased unfurnished, except for the following appliances, which remain the property of the Landlord: refrigerator, gas range, dishwasher, washer and dryer.

## 3. Term

The Lease begins on April 1, 2026 and ends on March 31, 2027 (the "Term").

If the Tenant wishes to renew, the Tenant must give written notice at least 60 days before the end of the Term, that is, no later than January 30, 2027. If neither party gives notice, the Lease converts to a month-to-month tenancy on the same terms, except that rent will increase by 4 percent.

## 4. Rent

4.1 The Tenant shall pay rent of $2,150 on the first day of each calendar month, by bank transfer to the account named in Schedule B.

4.2 Rent received more than five days late incurs a fee of $75, plus $10 for each further day the rent remains unpaid. The Landlord may waive this fee in writing.

4.3 Payments are applied first to outstanding fees, then to rent owed.

4.4 A returned or bounced payment incurs a charge of $40. After two returned payments, the Landlord may require all future payments by certified check.

## 5. Security Deposit

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,225, equal to one and a half months' rent, before receiving the keys.

5.2 The Landlord will hold the deposit in a separate interest-bearing account and return it, with interest, within 21 days after the Tenant moves out, less any lawful deductions for unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear and tear.

5.3 The Landlord will provide an itemized list of any deductions together with receipts or estimates.

5.4 The Tenant may not use the security deposit as payment of the last month's rent.

## 6. Utilities

The Landlord pays for water, sewer and trash collection. The Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, internet and any cable or streaming services, and must transfer these accounts into the Tenant's name before April 1, 2026.

## 7. Use and Occupancy

The Premises may be occupied only by the persons named as Tenant and their minor children. Guests may stay no more than 14 consecutive days, or 30 days in any 12-month period, without the Landlord's written consent.

The Tenant shall not operate a business that brings customers to the Premises, and shall not sublet the Premises or list them on any short-term rental service.

## 8. Pets

One cat or one dog under 40 pounds is permitted with a one-time, non-refundable pet fee of $300 and monthly pet rent of $35. The Tenant is liable for all damage caused by the pet and must keep dogs leashed in common areas.

## 9. Maintenance and Repairs

9.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and promptly report any leak, mold, pest problem or broken appliance to the property manager in writing.

9.2 The Landlord is responsible for repairs to the structure, plumbing, heating, electrical systems and the supplied appliances, except where damage is caused by the Tenant or their guests.

9.3 For emergencies such as flooding, gas smells or loss of heat in winter, the Tenant should call the 24-hour maintenance line at 555-0142. The Landlord will respond to emergencies within 24 hours and to other repair requests within 7 days.

9.4 The Tenant may not paint, install fixtures or make alterations without written approval. Small nail holes for hanging pictures are considered normal wear and tear.

## 10. Landlord's Entry

The Landlord may enter the Premises to inspect, make repairs or show the unit to prospective tenants or buyers, after giving at least 24 hours' written notice, between 8:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. In an emergency, the Landlord may enter without notice.

## 11. Insurance

The Tenant must keep renter's insurance with at least $100,000 in personal liability coverage throughout the Term and provide proof of coverage by April 15, 2026. The Landlord's insurance does not cover the Tenant's belongings.

## 12. Early Termination

12.1 The Tenant may end the Lease early by giving 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two months' rent ($4,300).

12.2 The early termination fee does not apply if the Tenant is called to active military duty, or in other cases where the law allows termination without penalty.

12.3 If the Landlord re-lets the Premises before the end of the notice period, the Landlord will refund the portion of rent paid for days the new tenant occupies the unit.

## 13. Move-Out

At the end of the Term, the Tenant shall return all keys, fobs and the parking permit, remove all belongings, and leave the Premises in the same condition as at move-in, apart from normal wear and tear. The parties will complete a joint move-out inspection within 3 days of the Tenant's departure.

## 14. Default

If the Tenant fails to pay rent within 10 days of the due date, or seriously breaches any other term of this Lease, the Landlord may give written notice to cure. If the problem is not fixed within 14 days of the notice, the Landlord may begin eviction proceedings as permitted by law.

## 15. Quiet Hours and Smoking

Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. Smoking or vaping of any substance is prohibited inside the building, on balconies and within 25 feet of any entrance.

## 16. Notices

All notices under this Lease must be in writing and delivered by hand, by mail to the addresses above, or by email to the addresses listed in Schedule A. Notices by email are effective on the next business day.

## 17. Entire Agreement

This Lease, together with Schedules A (contact details) and B (payment instructions), is the entire agreement between the parties. Any change must be made in writing and signed by both parties.

Signed on March 1, 2026.

Landlord: Harborview Property Holdings LLC, by Priya Natarajan, Property Manager

Tenant: Maya Okafor

Tenant: Daniel Reyes
